Carry the pint

Rest the phone flat on your palm, screen up, like a serving tray. Tap Start and hold steady for a moment while it finds level.

Small tilts won't budge the glass. Tip past the breakaway angle and it slides, and the faster it goes the harder you'll have to counter-tilt to catch it.

Keep the phone in portrait. If it slides the wrong way, use Tune → Flip.
