How it plays
  • Each player gets one turn per game. Your score is how many seconds you keep beer in the glass.
  • Rest the phone flat on your palm. Hold it level through the 3-2-1 so it can find level.
  • Rail bumps slop beer. Leaning on the rail dribbles it out. A hard hit knocks the glass over and ends your turn.
  • A new challenge pops up at the bottom every few seconds. Everyone else is the judge.
  • The bar gets slicker with every challenge, so nobody lasts forever.
